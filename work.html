About BUBU

BUBU Studio

Old Paphos, Cyprus. Studio in the making. Opening 2026.

BUBU Studio is a design studio for interior, product and aesthetics, and the home of drip tufting, the hand-tufting technique Jan Fechner developed. It brings together the tastes, interests and roots of its founders in a single home for beauty. The love for distinctive design did not begin with the studio, it began long before, in two different lives that were already moving towards each other. Jan comes from the design and marketing world, trained in how form meets message and how an idea becomes something people want to live with. Alizay studied art and carries with her a knowledge of design and interior shaped in Pakistan, where colour, pattern and craft belong to everyday life.

The name, playful and light, is where the story turns into a place. Everything started with a search for beautiful, high-contrast art for a children's room, not just something to hang on the wall but something a child could also live with on the floor. One question led to another, and the floor became the next obsession. Tufting. The shaping of textile. A craft that turns colour into surface, and surface into a place. What began in a nursery grew into a studio. Founded by Alizay and Jan in 2025, BUBU Studio now works where materials meet ideas, and where experimentation is the only method we trust.

Liquid Mountains is the first collection by Jan Fechner, and it pictures a Cypriot summer. Sun without mercy, heat that presses down on stone, a land so dry that almost nothing survives on it. Only small patches of green hold on, scattered across an otherwise bare and rocky landscape. The heat feels strong enough to melt even the mountains themselves, and that image of stone turning soft and fluid became the starting point for the collection. Into that heat Jan folds the colours of spring and the energy of sun and light, the pink and orange of a sunrise, the deep red of a sunset, the blue of the sea that never leaves the view in Cyprus. Poppy, bold colour meets bare, sunburnt ground. Form and colour are the language. Every piece is hand tufted wool, the fibre sourced from Northern Pakistan. Originals and studies are tufted by hand by Jan in the Paphos studio. Editions are produced with partner workshops in Pakistan and finished in Paphos. From Jan Fechner's hands into the world.

What drives us

  • Experimental by nature. We try, fail, and try again. That is where the good stuff lives.
  • Craft over perfection. Every imperfection tells a story worth keeping.
  • Community first. We love people. We love making things together.
  • Joy in the process. Making things should feel good, every step of the way.

Founders

Jan Fechner

Creative Director. Frankfurt Rhine Main and Old Paphos.

Jan is the artist behind the drip silhouettes. He grew up in the Rhine Main area and came into BUBU through the design and marketing world, where he learned how a message becomes a form and how a form finds its audience. At the meeting point between contemporary painting and textile sculpture he developed the BUBU visual language. Today he leads design direction, colour worlds and editions strategy, with the Cypriot summer always somewhere in the back of his mind.

Alizay

Co-Founder. Production and materials.

Alizay is from Karachi in Pakistan and has family roots in Lahore. She studied art and brings with her a knowledge of design and interior shaped in a place where colour, pattern and craft belong to everyday life. She is responsible for material quality, the tufting partnerships with Pakistani workshops and the full supply chain from raw fibre to a signed piece. What starts as wool in Northern Pakistan reaches the Paphos studio through her care and her eye.

Why BUBU

Around the Mediterranean classic carpets dominate as an accessory. Nobody runs contemporary artist rugs as their own category. BUBU closes that gap with limited editions, signed and numbered, made to be lived with rather than walked over and forgotten.

Aphrodite Sunset

A study by Jan Fechner. Part of the Liquid Mountains collection. Original study, not yet editioned.

This is the warmest end of the Liquid Mountains palette. Crimson, coral and saffron pour into a terracotta base and keep on pouring, the way a Cypriot sunset pours its last light over stone before the sea takes the colour away. A tufted study of that single hour, bold and poppy, rooted in nature.

Liquid Mountains

The collection by Jan Fechner. Studies in tufted wool.

Liquid Mountains is the first collection by Jan Fechner. It pictures a Cypriot summer, the heat that presses down on stone, the bare rocky land where only small patches of green survive, the sense that even the mountains could melt. Into that bareness Jan folds the colours of spring, the energy of sun and light, the blue of the sea. Bold and poppy colours meet sunburnt ground. Each study carries the three pillar drip silhouette that defines Jan Fechner's visual signature, made using drip tufting, the technique he developed. Hand tufted wool, the fibre sourced from Northern Pakistan. Every study is hand-tufted by Jan in the Paphos studio and finished and signed there.

Manufactory
M

Manufactory

Two ways a BUBU piece is made.

Every BUBU piece is hand tufted wool, the fibre sourced from Northern Pakistan, where Alizay's networks and family roots run deep. From there the work takes one of two paths, and the path is always named in the specs of the piece itself. Originals and studies are one of a kind works, hand tufted by Jan Fechner in the Paphos studio, each with a work number and a certificate of authenticity, finished and signed in Paphos. Editions are limited numbered runs, produced with partner workshops in Pakistan under Alizay's care, then finished, checked and signed in Paphos. The same standard on both ends, the same hand on the final cut. No guesswork for collectors or press.
